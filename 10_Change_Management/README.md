10 — Change Management

Purpose

This section demonstrates a practical Business Analyst change-control process for the simulated E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics project.

Contents

Change_Request_Log.xlsx — Change request register, impact assessment and summary.

Change_Management_Documentation.pdf — Change-control principles, workflow, impact assessment and interview talking point.

Change-Control Workflow

Raise change request

Assess business and technical impact

Review options, dependencies and effort

Obtain approval

Update requirements baseline and traceability

Coordinate implementation

Validate through UAT

Close and document the change

BA Responsibilities

Clarify the business need.

Identify affected requirements and stakeholders.

Perform impact analysis.

Maintain traceability.

Coordinate requirement updates.

Ensure UAT coverage reflects approved changes.

Communicate decisions and dependencies.

Portfolio Note

All project data, stakeholders, change requests and decisions are simulated/illustrative for portfolio and interview practice.
