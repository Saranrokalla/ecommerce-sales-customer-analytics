Requirements Documentation

This folder contains the Business Requirements Document (BRD), Functional Requirements Document (FRD), Business Rules Catalogue and Requirements Elicitation Questionnaire for the E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics case study.

Contents

BRD.pdf - business problem, objectives, scope and business requirements

FRD.pdf - functional/non-functional requirements, KPI definitions and acceptance criteria

Business_Rules.pdf - KPI and reporting logic

Requirements_Questionnaire.pdf - stakeholder interview and workshop questions

Portfolio note: This is a simulated case study using illustrative requirements and data.
